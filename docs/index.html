FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source.

Does it upload my images?

No. Your images stay on your computer.

What can it export?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, image sets, Chrome extensions, website icons, and store images.

What size should the source image be?

A 1024×1024 PNG works best.

Will it work on my computer?

Yes on Mac with Apple silicon, and on Windows. Download the installer and open it.
